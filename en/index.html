MESSAGE

More natural. More honest.
Looking after yourself shouldn't feel like going without.

Thank you for stopping by. I run Sora Tobu Studio, where I teach two things: yoga rooted in the breath, and cooking with local vegetables and Eastern spices. To me they're the same practice in different forms — both are about learning to listen to your body.

It's easy, in a busy life, to stop noticing what your body is telling you. I'd rather start from what you feel than from theory. With the quiet and the harvests of Koshu to help, I hope we can build that practice together.

Miko Omiya — Founder, Sora Tobu Studio

Philosophy
OUR PHILOSOPHY

Three things
we hold close

Follow nature's rhythm

We work with the season and the land rather than against them, adjusting to how your body feels on the day.

Sensation before theory

Knowing something rarely changes it. We start with what you feel in the body, and build understanding from there.

Quiet, woven into daily life

You don't need a special place or a special occasion. What you learn here is meant to come home with you.
