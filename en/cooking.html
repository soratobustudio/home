COOKING CLASS

Good food,
felt from the inside.

Nourish

Less technique. More respect for the ingredient.

We cook with vegetables grown around Koshu City and spices from across Asia, in dishes meant to nourish you from the inside. No special skills or expensive equipment needed — the focus is on simple methods that bring out what seasonal produce already has in it.

We don't just cook and finish there. We talk through why each combination works for the body, so you can keep using the idea in your own kitchen.

01What the Class Is Like

  • Built around vegetables grown near Koshu City
  • Menus that use Eastern spices like turmeric and cumin
  • Hands-on — you'll be cooking, not watching
  • You'll eat what you make, right there in class

02Who It's For

  • You'd like your everyday meals to be a little gentler on you
  • You're curious about spices and fermented foods but not sure where to start
  • You want more of the season, and more of where you live, in what you eat
