YOGA CLASS · For Experienced Practitioners

For those ready
to go deeper.

Practice

Less about the shape. More about what you feel.

This class is for anyone with at least a year of practice behind them. It's a place to sharpen your breath and alignment, and take the experience you've already built somewhere deeper.

We don't teach the fundamentals here. Classes stay small so we can work with each person's body and sticking points — a good fit if you're looking to refine a practice you already have.

01What the Class Is Like

  • Careful, detailed work on breathwork and asana
  • Small classes of 6–10, with guidance suited to your body
  • We cover not just how to move, but why it works that way
  • Held in a naturally lit studio in Koshu City

02Who It's For

  • You practice regularly and want to move to the next stage
  • You want to revisit habits you've picked up on your own
  • You're working toward teacher certification, or simply want to widen your practice
