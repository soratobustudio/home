Corporate

Wellbeing isn't just down to the individual.

We build tailored programmes for companies — wellness menus for staff canteens, sessions for training days, on-site yoga. We come at it from two directions: what people eat, and how they use their bodies.

Nothing off the shelf. We design each programme after hearing what's actually going on in your organisation.

01What We Do

  • Wellness menu development for staff canteens and cafeterias(any company size)
  • On-site yoga for training days and company events(teams of 10 or fewer)
  • Wellbeing sessions combining food and movement
  • Recipe development and consulting

02Problems We Help With
