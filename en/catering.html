Mandala

One dish, spread right across the platter.

A mandala is a symmetrical pattern built outward from a circle. Our Biryani Mandala lays colourful ingredients in rings across one large platter — a dish made to be looked at, shared, and eaten together.

Private celebrations, company gatherings — wherever the people who matter are in one room, we'll bring it to the table.
